01 / About

Data pipelines that ship — and stay clean

I'm a Senior Data Engineer and Data Migration Consultant with 7+ years turning messy enterprise source data into trustworthy, production-ready datasets. I've owned end-to-end delivery on SAP ERP → S/4HANA migrations, built the SQL and validation layers that get business sign-off, and shipped self-service reporting that lets operational teams answer their own questions.

My work sits where SQL depth meets data quality discipline: complex transformations with CTEs, window functions and stored procedures; automated pre/post-load reconciliation; and profiling that catches problems before they hit production. I'm actively expanding into the modern stack — Python, dbt, Apache Airflow and AWS — and use AI developer tooling (Claude Code, Codex) day to day.
